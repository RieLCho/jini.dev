#### 'Hayaku' is a Android porting of iOS 'Tap to Tweet' Button.

Nowadays we have many steps to send a tweet.

**Just slide down your status bar and write down your momentary thoughts!**

### How to use Hayaku

1. Scroll Down Status Bar
2. Write Your Tweet
3. Press Enter

### Used Libraries

- Twitter4j
- Glide
